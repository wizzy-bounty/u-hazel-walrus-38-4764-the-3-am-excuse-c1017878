One line, under 200 characters: the most creative excuse a hardware wallet gives for refusing to sign a transaction at 3 AM. The wallet's angle is simple — 3 AM is when the scammers clock in and the owner's judgment clocks out, so until sunrise, it signs for no one.

## The entry

> At 3 AM the scammers clock in and your judgment clocks out — so until sunrise, I sign for no one.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python standard library only, deterministic, no network) validates the line against the competition gate — UTF-8 text, 1–200 characters, a single line, not whitespace-only — and writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).
